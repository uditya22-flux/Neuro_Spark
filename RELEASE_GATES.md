# Private-beta release gates

Block release until all of the following are complete:

- Guardian verification provider or an approved DigiLocker-compatible path is contracted and integrated.
- India privacy/consent copy, subprocessor terms, retention/deletion workflow, and legal review are approved.
- A safeguarding owner, restricted review workflow, response SLA, and escalation resources are live.
- An OpenAI API project, server-side key, commercial terms, and approved Modified Abuse Monitoring or Zero Data Retention arrangement are verified; development remains on the fake provider.
- AWS Mumbai account, domain, Apple Developer, and Google Play accounts are available.
- Production secrets are held in Secrets Manager; no development fallback keys remain.
- Authorization, cross-tenant, revoked-session, database-trigger, retention, purge, taxonomy, accessibility, and bias fixtures pass against real managed dependencies.
- Android and iOS integration runs cover consent, configuration changes, offline recovery, safe exits, accessibility, and audio interruption.
- Backup lifecycle deletion and object-store purge evidence are reviewed.
