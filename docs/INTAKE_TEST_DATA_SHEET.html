MindBridge — Field Test Intake Data Sheet

Give this sheet to guardians during tablet testing. Enter the values in the app intake screens, then check that customization appears in the strength funnel.

App login (field test)
Tap Field test sign-in on the login screen (one tap).
Or email: mindbridge.pilot.test@gmail.com · Password: MindBridge2026!

Flow: Sign in → Consent (check box) → Intake (3 steps) → Strength funnel (10 layers) → Child play

ISAA score guide (Clinical step — sliders 1 to 5)

ScoreMeaning in app
1Minimal support need in that domain
2Mild
3Moderate (default / balanced test)
4Elevated support need
5High support need

Parent step — pick exact options from the app

FieldOptions in app
Hyper-fixationTrains & Vehicles · Space & Astronomy · Geometry & Patterns · Animals & Nature · Clocks & Numbers
Sound triggersHigh-frequency beeps · Sudden loud chime · Background music loops · Voice-over narration · Applause or cheering
Visual triggersRapid flashing · High contrast red/yellow · Parallax scrolling · Floating UI elements · Busy animated backgrounds
VibrationPrefers haptics · No vibrations · Neutral

What to look for after intake (Environment preview + funnel)

  • Environment preview (step 3): theme skin, audio mode, instruction style, haptics on/off
  • Strength funnel banner: “Customized for [child name] (ISAA + parent intake)” with chips (Picture cards, Visual-only hints, etc.)
  • Each theme: Step 1 = try activity (tap/stack/sort) → Step 2 = rate how fun (slider unlocks after trying)
  • App bar: Layer X of 10 (use Field test sign-in, not Hospital demo)
